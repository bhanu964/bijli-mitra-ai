Problem Statement & Ground Truth SLIDE 03 / 12

The Citizen Crisis: The Electricity Bill Shock Epidemic

Over 1.8 Crore households in Telangana receive spot-billing slips printed from handheld dot-matrix terminals. The bills are abbreviated, cryptic, and opaque.

⚠️ The Telescopic Slab Re-Rating Trap

Consuming 201 units doesn't just bill 1 extra unit at a higher slab; it re-rates the consumer's entire monthly usage under LT-I(B)(ii), causing a sudden ₹350+ bill jump.

⚠️ Gruha Jyothi "Cliff Effect" (200 Units)

Under G.O.Ms.No.7, consuming ≤200 units gives a 100% Zero Bill. Exceeding 200 units by just 1 unit wipes out the entire government subsidy immediately.

⚠️ Arrears & Defective Meter Jumps

Consumers cannot tell whether large bills stem from current usage, multi-year True-Up recoveries, or erroneous defective meter leaps (e.g. ₹11.41 Crores on a village pump).
