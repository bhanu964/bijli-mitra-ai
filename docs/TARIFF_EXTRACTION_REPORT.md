### 1. Structural Architecture of Telangana LT-I Domestic Billing

The Telangana Domestic (LT-I) tariff follows a **tiered bracket model with intra-bracket telescopic slabs**:
1. The **consumer's total monthly consumption** determines which tier group applies:
   - **Tier A (LT-I(A)):** Total consumption $\le 100$ units/month.
   - **Tier B(i) (LT-I(B)(i)):** Total consumption between $101$ and $200$ units/month.
   - **Tier B(ii) (LT-I(B)(ii)):** Total consumption $> 200$ units/month.
2. Once the tier group is determined, billing within that tier is **telescopic across defined sub-slabs**.
3. **The "Bracket Shift" Effect:** When consumption crosses 100 units (e.g. from 100 to 101 units), the rate applied to the *first 100 units* shifts from Tier A rates (Rs. 1.95 for 0-50, Rs. 3.10 for 51-100) to Tier B(i) rates (Rs. 3.40 for 0-100). Similarly, crossing 200 units (e.g. 200 to 201 units) shifts the base rate for the first 200 units to Rs. 5.10.
4. Explaining this cliff/bracket shift clearly in English and Telugu is one of the primary value propositions of BIJLI MITRA AI.

### 4. Electricity Duty (Telangana State Electricity Duty Act, 1939)

- **Standard Domestic Rate:** 6 paise per unit (**Rs. 0.06 / kWh**).
- Applies directly to all billed energy consumption units.
- Must be itemized separately on the electricity bill.

---

### 5. Delayed Payment Surcharge (DPS)

- **LT-I(A) (0–100 units):** Flat **Rs. 10.00 per month** if payment is made after the due date.
- **LT-I(B) (>100 units):** Flat **Rs. 25.00 per month** if payment is made after the due date.

---

### 6. True-Up Recovery Order (05.10.2026) Rules

- **Effective Billing Commencement:** November 2026 Current Consumption (CC) bills.
- **Applicability:** All domestic (LT-I) and non-domestic service connections active in FY 2022-23 and FY 2023-24.
- **Monthly Quantum ($Q_{monthly}$):**
  $$Q_{monthly} = \frac{\text{Total Annual kWh Consumed in FY 2022-23 + FY 2023-24}}{12}$$
- **Recovery Rates:**
  - Month 1 (Nov 2026): **Rs. 0.30 per unit** of $Q_{monthly}$.
  - Subsequent Months (Dec 2026 onwards): **Rs. 0.50 per unit** of $Q_{monthly}$.
- **Duration:** 29 months for TGSPDCL; 26 months for TGNPDCL.
- **Legal Mandate:** Must be itemized separately; never bundled into Energy Charges.

---

### 7. Gruha Jyothi Zero-Bill Eligibility Rules

- **Criterion:** Monthly units $\le 200$, White Ration Card linked, Aadhaar linked, Praja Palana registered.
- **Subsidy:** 100% of bill amount credited by GoTS under Section 65 of Electricity Act, 2003. Net payable by consumer = **Rs. 0.00**.
- **Crucial Rule:** If consumption is 201 units, subsidy is **Rs. 0.00** and consumer pays full LT-I(B)(ii) bill of ~Rs. 1,500+!
